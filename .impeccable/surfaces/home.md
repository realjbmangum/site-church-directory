# Home

Visitor mode: persuade

Audience: people new to Charlotte looking for a church they can visit this Sunday.
Job: search by name, place, or denomination and trust the service time.
Action: submit the search, which opens the map with those filters. The featured card opens that church.
Proof: the earliest Sunday service in the directory, with the church’s own times and the last-checked date.
Constraints: match the approved photographic mockup. Plain English, no em dashes, no side accent borders. Keep routes, data, queries, SEO, the denomination filter, and the admin desk.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a Sunday morning you can check. It refuses the spruce timetable band and the cream brochure. A photograph of a white country church carries the first screen, and the earliest service sits in a white card you can open.

OWN-WORLD: Golden-hour hills, a white church, and a warm sky. Transparent header, dark ink, Source Serif 4 for titles and times, Source Sans 3 for reading. White rounded cards, soft offset shadows, outlined buttons, warm paper under the photograph. No spruce fill, no terracotta, no side bars.

STORY: A newcomer reads the offer, searches by name or denomination, and sees a real earliest Sunday time with the church, the place, the service list, and the date we checked it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed church photograph. Header over the sky: serif wordmark at left, nav centered, “Add a church” at right, a thin light rule under it. Left: serif headline “Find a Sunday service you can check.”, a sans lede, and a white search card with name and denomination. Right: a white card with a large serif time, a small-caps caption under that time, the church name, place and family, an inset of service times, the last-checked date, and an outlined “Open the listing” button. Lower on the photograph: “More Sunday times” and a row of white time cards. The search is the primary action.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) “Redo the visual design of the whole public site to look like the attached mockup image”, a full-bleed golden-hour church photograph with the earliest Sunday service in a floating white card. Signature interaction: the featured card settles 10px into place, already visible, shadow easing with it. Reduced motion removes the move.
