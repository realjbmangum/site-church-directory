# Home

Visitor mode: persuade

Audience: people new to Charlotte looking for a church they can visit this Sunday.
Job: search by name, place, or denomination and trust the service time.
Action: submit the search, which opens the map with those filters.
Proof: one real listing with the church’s own words and the last-checked date.
Constraints: plain English, no em dashes, no side accent borders, no church clip art. Keep routes, data, and SEO.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a Sunday timetable. It refuses the cream brochure with a serif headline, a terracotta button, and a row of equal stat cards. The offer is a service time you can check, and the search is on the page.

OWN-WORLD: Spruce destination band, cool paper, white cards with full borders. Familjen Grotesk sets titles and times. Atkinson Hyperlegible sets the proof and body. Chips, rules, and one shadow on the map popup. Map stops use the same spruce. No crosses, no side bars, no second accent.

STORY: A newcomer understands that listings are copied from church sites, searches, and opens a church whose time and quote are already on the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A spruce band holds the wordmark. Under it, the same spruce field splits into the headline and working search on the left, and a white proof card on the right: a large Sunday time, the church name, a quote, the last-checked date, and a link to the listing. The search button is the primary action.

FORM: Sunday timetable, position 3 of 7, seed:934bc458d3b3fb9a. Signature interaction: the map redraws its stops when the family filter changes, with a short color fade that snaps off under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
